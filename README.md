💙 Meet Your Virtual Companion 💙
</div>
<br/>
 ** My Virtual Doraemon ** is a playful, nostalgic web companion that brings everyone's favorite 22nd-century robotic cat to life! Feed him yummy snacks, activate magical gadgets, and play mini-games right in your browser.
<br/><br/>
<div align="center">
✨ 4D Magic Pocket Gadgets ✨
</div>
<br/>
* 🥞 ** Dorayaki Feeder ** =  
  Feed Doraemon his favorite snacks and watch his happy reaction animations and energy meter rise!
<br/>
* 🚁 ** Take-Copter Flight ** =  
  Equip the propeller and watch Doraemon fly smoothly across the screen using Canvas physics.
<br/>
* 🚪 ** Anywhere Door ** =  
  Open the iconic pink door to instantly teleport across fun, colorful virtual destinations.
<br/>
* 🕹️ ** Arcade Mini-Games ** =  
  Play built-in browser challenges to earn points and unlock secret futuristic gadgets.
<br/><br/>
